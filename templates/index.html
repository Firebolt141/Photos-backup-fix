Fix Missing Dates
Walks a backup drive organised as year/month/day and writes the folder's date into every photo or video that has none — in place. Files that already have a date are never changed; ones whose date disagrees with their folder are listed. Works on JPEG, PNG, HEIC, RAW and MP4/MOV.
